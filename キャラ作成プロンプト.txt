=====================================================
 キャラクター追加用の画像生成プロンプト
=====================================================

■ 使い方
  1. Gemini などの画像生成AIに、参考にしたいキャラクターの
     画像を1枚添付する(人でも動物でもマスコットでもOK)
  2. 下の英語プロンプトをそのまま貼り付けて生成
  3. できあがったシートをClaudeに渡せば、透過処理・4分割・
     アプリへの配置までやってもらえます
     (背景が市松模様の「なんちゃって透過」でも処理できます)

■ カスタマイズのヒント
  ・白衣が不要なら「Dress the character ...」の行を削除
  ・ポーズを変えたいときは THE 4 POSES の各行を書き換え
    (アプリの割り当ては 左から idle / happy / stretch / rest)

-----------------------------------------------------
 ↓↓ ここから下をコピーして貼り付け ↓↓
-----------------------------------------------------

Create a character variation sheet based on the attached reference character.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row containing exactly 4 full-body versions of the SAME character
- Evenly spaced, with clear empty gaps between the figures (no overlapping)
- All 4 figures at the same scale, feet aligned on the same invisible ground line
- Fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). No scenery, no frame, no text, no watermark, no UI

CHARACTER
- Keep the attached character's identity: same face, hairstyle, hair color, eye color and overall vibe
- Chibi anime style, soft rounded shapes, clean bold outlines, warm friendly colors (cute mobile game mascot look)
- Dress the character in a white lab coat (knee length, worn open) in all 4 poses, with their own clothes visible underneath

THE 4 POSES (left to right)
1. IDLE - standing upright and relaxed, neutral gentle expression, arms at the sides
2. HAPPY - cheerful celebration: big smile, happy closed eyes, small hearts and sparkles floating around the figure
3. STRETCH - both arms raised high in a big stretch, mouth open as if exhaling, refreshed and energetic
4. REST - sitting down and dozing, eyes closed, cheek resting on one hand, sleepy and cozy

QUALITY
- High resolution, each figure at least 400px tall
- A soft subtle shadow ellipse under each figure's feet is fine; nothing else may touch the figures
